WHO WE ARE

Global Value Chain E-commerce

Our platform is designed to connect meaningful purchases and consumer participation with opportunities for brands and communities.

Rather than treating shopping as a one-time transaction, MYV aims to build stronger connections among brands, consumers, and prosumers. We help promising Korean brands tell their stories and explore growth opportunities at home and internationally.

Operated by V Labs Inc., MYV brings value-driven curation and a relationship-based commerce approach together.
